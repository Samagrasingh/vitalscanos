The engine

Built on signal science

Every check-in is a small signal-processing pipeline that runs entirely on your phone — and stays there.

Camera rPPG

The camera picks up the subtle colour change each heartbeat causes in your skin.

Runs on your phone

Frames are analysed in memory, never saved or sent — the app has no internet permission.

Quality first

If lighting or movement makes the signal unreliable, it asks you to try again instead of showing you a number.

Personal trends

Check-ins build your own baseline, so changes are compared with you — not a population average.

Four apps in one

One install, four ways to look at your signals

VitalScanOS ships as a small suite — a daily check-in plus three labs for exploring what your camera can read.

Camera Vitals

Hold still and look at the camera for a 20–30 second scan. VitalScanOS estimates:

  • Pulse (heart rate)
  • Heart-rate variability (HRV)
  • Breathing rate and stress level
  • Plain-language insights compared with your own baseline

Cabin Demo

A demo of in-car wellness sensing: a single camera produces live wellness estimates for a driver and a passenger at the same time.

  • Each person is tracked to their own seat
  • Live estimates update as the session runs
  • A simulated mode is included for trying it anywhere

Emotion Lab

On-device facial-expression insights. After a short neutral calibration, Emotion Lab shows how your facial signals shift.

  • Calibrates to your neutral face first
  • Shows shifts in expression signals over a session
  • Processed entirely on your phone

Physiology Lab

Watch live pulse and breathing trends — follow them during breathing exercises, rest, or activity.

  • Live trend view while a session runs
  • Use it during breathing exercises or at rest
  • Session summaries stored on your device

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is VitalScanOS a medical device?

No. This app is for general wellness and informational use only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a doctor for medical advice.

Does my video leave my phone?

No. Camera frames are processed in memory and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data even if it wanted to.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with a front camera. Works best in good, even lighting with the phone held still. Not guaranteed on every device.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and nothing to log in to.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → “Delete all my data”, or simply uninstall the app. Everything is stored on your device, so deleting it there removes it completely.
