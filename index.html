VitalScanOS

Camera-based wellness check-ins for Android. Your phone's camera reads subtle changes in your face to estimate pulse, heart-rate variability, breathing and stress — all processed on your device, with nothing sent anywhere.

Camera Vitals

Hold still and look at the camera for a 20–30 second scan. VitalScanOS estimates:

  • Pulse (heart rate)
  • Heart-rate variability (HRV)
  • Breathing rate
  • Stress level

All results are wellness estimates to help you notice trends — not measurements for medical use.

Cabin Demo

A demo of in-car wellness sensing: a single camera produces live wellness estimates for a driver and a passenger at the same time, each tracked to their own seat.

Emotion Lab

On-device facial-expression insights. After a short neutral calibration, Emotion Lab shows how your facial signals shift — processed entirely on your phone.

Physiology Lab

Watch live pulse and breathing trends — follow them during breathing exercises, rest, or activity.
