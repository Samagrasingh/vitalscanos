The approach

Built the same way as VitalScanOS

On-device by default

Frames are processed in memory on the device; nothing needs to leave it.

Quality gating

An unreliable signal prompts a retry — never a guessed number.

Flexible UI

Your screens, your style — or we can supply ours as a starting point.

Personal baselines

Estimates are compared with each user’s own history.

The signals

What it can estimate

Pulse

Heart rate at rest.

Heart-rate variability

Beat-to-beat variation, a recovery signal.

Breathing rate

Breaths per minute while still.

Stress level

A 1–10 index built from pulse and HRV.

All are wellness estimates, not measurements for medical use.
