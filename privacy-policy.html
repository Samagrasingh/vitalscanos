VitalScanOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

VitalScanOS is a wellness app operated by Flace Multimedia Pvt Ltd, Rangbari, Kota, Rajasthan, India — the data fiduciary for this app under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP). Contact: samagra29@gmail.com (company) · thethinkingpixel@gmail.com (support)

No account, no sign-in

VitalScanOS has no accounts. You never sign in, and there is nothing to log in to.

On-device processing only

The app does not have the internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere — there is no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Camera frames never leave memory

To estimate pulse, breathing, heart-rate-variability-based stress and expression signals, the app analyses live camera frames in memory, on the device. Frames are never saved and never transmitted.

What stays on your device

Derived numbers from check-ins (pulse, breathing, stress estimates), optional smartwatch pulse reference readings, lab session summaries, your consent record, and settings are stored only on your device. They are kept until you delete them: "Delete all my data" in Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything.

Purchases

If you subscribe, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details; it receives only the purchase state (active, pending or cancelled) from the Play Store on your device.

Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Consent: the app only runs after you agree on the consent screen; you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings. Purpose limitation: data is used only to show you your wellness estimates. You may access your data (Export my data in Settings), correct it (add a new reference reading or delete history), and erase it (Delete all my data). For grievance redressal contact our grievance officer: Ashok Raj Singh, thethinkingpixel@gmail.com. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Export

You can export your data as a file to a location you choose. Export is always initiated by you.

Children

VitalScanOS is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Wellness disclaimer

This app is for general wellness and informational use only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a doctor for medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app. Experimental features may ask for fresh consent before they run.
