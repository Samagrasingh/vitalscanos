VitalScanOS, operated by Flace Multimedia Pvt Ltd, provides camera-based wellness estimates (pulse, breathing, stress and related signals) processed entirely on your device.

This app is for general wellness and informational use only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a doctor for medical advice. Never use the app in an emergency or to make medical decisions.
